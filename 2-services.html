Our services

Everything an international student needs from first question to first semester, in English or Arabic.

1

Application guidance

Step-by-step help with university applications, documents, deadlines, and submission strategy.

2

Student visa assistance

F-1 and M-1 visa guidance, SEVIS registration, interview preparation, and maintaining your status.

3

Choosing universities

Hand-picked options that fit your academic goals and budget, including community college pathways.

4

Test preparation

Direction for TOEFL, IELTS, and Duolingo English Test preparation and study plans.

5

Scholarships and costs

Help finding scholarships and building a realistic budget for tuition and living costs in the US.

6

Document translation

Translation and notarization guidance for transcripts, diplomas, and recommendation letters.

7

Pre-departure orientation

Housing, health insurance, travel, and what daily life in the US looks like before you fly.

8

Academic counseling

Course selection, transfer credits, and staying on track after you arrive.

Trained for this work

Our adviser completed the ICEF US Agent Training Course and earned the ICEF US Course Graduate (USCG) credential in September 2025 (certificate USCG #720). ICEF training covers US education, student visas, and ethical recruitment standards for education agents.

Start with a conversation

Tell us where you want to study and what you need. We reply in English or Arabic, usually within one business day.
