About us

Bilingual guidance for international students and their families, from Hayward, California.

Who we are

Silicon Valley Agency for International Students guides international students and their families through US university admissions: choosing the right school, preparing applications, and getting ready for the student visa. We work in English and Arabic, and we welcome parents into every conversation.

We are based in Hayward, California, in the San Francisco Bay Area, and we work with students and families wherever they are.

How we work

Every student starts with a personal consultation. Then we agree on a clear plan for your goals, your budget, and your timeline, and we work that plan with you step by step, from applications to pre-departure orientation and support after you arrive.

Trained for this work

Our adviser completed the ICEF US Agent Training Course and earned the ICEF US Course Graduate (USCG) credential in September 2025 (certificate USCG #720). ICEF training covers US education, student visas, and ethical recruitment standards for education agents.
